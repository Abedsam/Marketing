# Direct Line Marketing — BRIEF

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The brand direction, palette,
and typography were already fixed by the user across the preceding
conversation (a pasted "Dala" style reference, adapted onto a white canvas
with the real company's wine-red logo woven back in); this pass applies
scroll-craft's structural discipline and one signature move on top of that
already-approved system rather than re-opening those decisions.

1. **Vibe**: monolithic type, quiet confidence, one violet accent. References:
   the user-supplied Dala style reference; Linear/Vercel-style flat premium-minimal.
2. **Journey** (index.html): Start (hero claim) → Leistungen (three services,
   no cards) → Partner & Kunden (trust marquee) → Team-Bewertungen (real
   Google reviews from employees/applicants) → Kontakt (close).
3. **Energy curve**: calm, declarative hero → steady, even-paced services →
   light social-proof marquees → resolves on a plain, quiet CTA. No single
   loud peak was requested; this is an editorial-toned B2B site, not a
   cinematic launch.
4. **Feeling curve**:
   - Start: confident, direct ("Ihre Direktverbindung")
   - Leistungen: competent, concrete (three plain services, no persuasion gloss)
   - Partner: reassured (recognizable names drifting past)
   - Team: warmed (real employee voices, not manufactured testimonials)
   - Kontakt: resolved, low-pressure (one button, one phone number)
   Peak: none engineered as a cinematic moment — the page's honesty (real
   Google reviews, real logo, real address) is the substance, not a single
   staged beat.
5. **Signature move**: "Direktverbindung"-Trace — a fixed rail at the bottom
   of the viewport draws a literal line as the visitor scrolls, filling with
   the brand violet and lighting up a labelled stop (Start / Leistungen /
   Partner / Team / Kontakt) as each section is reached. Doubles as
   jump-navigation. Independent of GSAP, so it degrades gracefully.
6. **Range**: premium-minimal, per the Dala reference.
7. **Structure**: distinct scenes/sections (multi-page business site: index,
   Über uns, Leistungen, Team, Kontakt), not a single continuous world —
   correct for a real service business with real pages to navigate, not a
   one-shot narrative product launch.
8. **Assets on hand**: the real company logo (assets/logo.png, extracted from
   a user-supplied screenshot); no photography or footage. No KIE_AI_API_KEY
   configured and no ffmpeg build available in this environment, so no
   generated imagery or video was produced — the hero visual is a
   code-driven particle constellation (Three.js), not photography.

**Tell-someone sentence**: "It's the site with the little violet line at the
bottom that fills in as you scroll and shows you exactly where you are."
